## Singularity
Singularity is a 2D space adventure game where its primary objective is to survive in a blackhole as spaceship and to solve puzzles. Carve out your path in the cosmos by dodging obstacles, collecting key letters, and solving riddles.

## Mechanics
Your ship will be constantly sucked in by the black hole, so you will need to use your thruster to keep yourself out of the event horizon. This thruster requires fuel to use, which can be obtained by shooting certain types of space debris. Many different obstacles will block your path and you must maneuver or destroy them. Getting hit by a piece of debris or asteroid will cause your ship to lose 1 health point (3 to start). Buffs will randomly appear and can give effects such as recovered health, increased weapon fire rate, forcefield, refuel, etc. In each level, there will a master word comprised of key letters. These key letters that make up this master word will slowly appear while playing and will complete the word when all are collected. These letters are not in order, so when all the letters are collected, you will have to guess the word based on these letters and a clue. Guessing the master word correctly will allow you to move on to the next level, which will be harder than the previous, and will introduce new mechanics. If you fail to complete the master word correctly, you will lose the game, and will receive a score, which is based on the amount of stages completed, the amount of time they are completed in, and the amount of objects destroyed.
